FINSHARE PLATFORM

About Us

FinShare (finshareapp.in) is a referral and partner offer discovery platform designed to help registered users explore available referral programs, share eligible links, and monitor their lead status and payout records in one organized interface.

Our goal is to provide partners with a clear, structured, and easy-to-use workspace where they can view active campaigns, read program requirements, track submitted or referred leads, and manage withdrawal requests subject to platform rules.

How FinShare Works

  • Offer Discovery: Users can browse listed referral and partner programs across categories such as banking, demat, credit/financial products, and promotional campaigns.
  • Sharing & Lead Tracking: Users can share referral or campaign links and check lead updates, confirmation statuses, and pending/confirmed figures within their account dashboard.
  • Payout Management: Once eligible rewards are confirmed under campaign and platform rules, users can review their balance and submit payout requests according to applicable terms.

Important Platform & Role Disclosure

FinShare is not a bank, Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), stock exchange, registered broker, loan provider, or investment advisor. FinShare does not underwrite loans, issue credit cards or bank accounts, or guarantee financial returns. All products, offers, eligibility criteria, approval decisions, and commission/reward payouts are governed by the respective third-party partner brands or affiliate networks and their official terms.

Commitment to Transparency & Compliance

We encourage ethical sharing practices and accurate information. Users must never make misleading claims, guarantee approvals or earnings, or engage in spam or fraudulent lead activity. Offer availability, reward rates, verification timelines, and campaign terms may change without prior notice based on partner updates.
