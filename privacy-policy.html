PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026 • Domain: https://finshareapp.in

FinShare ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information shared with us through the FinShare website and partner platform (finshareapp.in). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and how you can manage or request deletion of your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: Details such as your name, email address, mobile number, and partner profile identifier when you register or sign in.
  • Lead & Referral Tracking Information: Referral/partner identifiers, selected offer or product details, lead status updates, and lead entry information submitted for referral tracking.
  • Payout & Bank Details: Bank account holder name, account number, IFSC code, and bank name that you voluntarily provide to process eligible withdrawal requests.
  • Technical & Usage Data: Standard session, authentication, device/browser, and diagnostic data required to keep the service secure and functional.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your FinShare partner account and authenticate sessions.
  • To record, display, and track referral leads, offer status, earnings summaries, and withdrawal requests.
  • To process payouts, respond to support requests, and prevent fraud, duplicate/invalid submissions, or misuse.
  • To maintain platform reliability, security, and compliance with applicable policies and laws.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information may be processed through infrastructure and cloud service providers (such as authentication and database hosting services) strictly to operate FinShare, or shared where necessary to verify referral activity or comply with legal obligations.

When you or a referred user follow a referral link to a third-party brand, financial institution, or partner platform, that third party's own privacy policy and terms govern how data is collected and handled on their site or app.

4. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard account and transaction records. Data is retained for as long as needed to provide your account services, maintain lead/payout records, resolve disputes, or meet legal and compliance requirements.

5. Your Choices, Access & Account/Data Deletion

You may review or update certain profile and bank details inside your account dashboard. If you wish to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated personal data, please contact us at support@finshareapp.in or visit our Contact Us page. Please note that certain records may be retained where required by law, fraud prevention, or audit/payout rules.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect service or policy updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact support@finshareapp.in.
